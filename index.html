About

Engineer & creative,
one continuous process.

I'm Oluwatobi Ayodele, a Web Developer, Web Designer, and Social Media Manager based in Lagos, Nigeria. I create modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites that help businesses establish a strong digital presence and connect with their audience.

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, and modern web design principles to build websites that are visually appealing, functional, fast, and mobile-friendly. Beyond development, I help brands grow online through social media management, content creation, creative design, and digital marketing — combining technology, design, and strategy into digital experiences that don't just look good, but serve a purpose.
